MINISTERIAL SUBMISSION

Subject: Approval Required for AI Safety Summit Attendance

Date: 5 February 2026

SUMMARY

This submission seeks your approval to attend the International AI Safety Summit in Paris on 15-17 March 2026.

BACKGROUND

The AI Safety Summit brings together 45 nations to discuss frontier AI risk governance. DSIT has been leading UK participation in the international AI safety agenda since the Bletchley Declaration.

KEY POINTS

1. The summit represents a significant opportunity to shape international AI governance frameworks
2. Your presence would reinforce UK leadership in AI safety
3. Several bilateral meetings are proposed with EU and US counterparts
4. Costs are estimated at £3,500 for travel and accommodation

RECOMMENDATION

I recommend that you:
a) Approve attendance at the AI Safety Summit
b) Approve the proposed bilateral meetings
c) Note the associated costs
