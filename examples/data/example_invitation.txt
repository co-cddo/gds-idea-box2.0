Dear Minister,

I am writing on behalf of the Royal Society to invite you to deliver the keynote address at the
International AI Governance Forum, taking place on 20-21 March 2026 at the Royal Society, London.

The Forum will bring together leading policymakers, researchers and industry leaders from over
30 countries to discuss the next steps in AI safety regulation following the Bletchley Declaration.
Key topics include frontier model evaluation, international regulatory alignment, and responsible
AI deployment in public services.

We would be honoured if you could deliver a 20-minute keynote on the morning of 20 March,
followed by a Q&A session. Alternatively, if your schedule does not permit the 20th, we have
a slot available on the afternoon of 21 March.

Confirmed speakers include Professor Dame Angela McLean (Government Chief Scientific Adviser),
Brad Smith (Vice Chair, Microsoft), and Demis Hassabis (CEO, Google DeepMind).

Please could we have confirmation by 28 February 2026 so that we can finalise the programme.

For any questions, please contact Sarah Johnson at sarah.johnson@royalsociety.org or
call 020 7451 2500.

With best wishes,
